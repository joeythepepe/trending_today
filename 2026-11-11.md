## Top 10 AI News Today

1. Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to paying developers and enterprises through the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Gemini for business at the same introductory price as Gemini 4 Argon ($2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens), and is rolling it out to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity, the Gemini app, and AI Mode in Search. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)
2. Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, an agentic formal-verification framework whose Gemini Carbon-powered agents generated over six million lines of Lean proofs to show that Signal’s compiled cryptographic code meets key security properties, completing in four weeks a task that historically takes expert teams years. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/)
3. OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, letting companies claim and verify a profile in Business Hub, add product feeds, and view aggregated engagement insights, and added Business Agents for eligible U.S. ecommerce and online service businesses. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt)
4. At a San Francisco event on Tuesday, Anthropic put Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards into beta on all paid plans and Claude Meetings into beta on Team and Enterprise plans, and made Claude Data generally available to all paid plans. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030709/anthropic-has-its-own-ai-powered-office-suite-now)
5. Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2.5, its most capable vision-language-action model to date, and said Apptronik’s Apollo 2 humanoid used the models to kit automotive parts on active Mercedes-Benz production lines in Hungary and Germany with a 96% success rate. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-5-accelerates-real-world-physical-intelligence/)
6. Anthropic set an IPO price range of $70 to $78 per share in an updated filing Tuesday, implying a fully diluted valuation of $1.404 trillion at the top of the range, and said the offering of 825 million Class A shares is expected to raise $57.75 billion to $64.35 billion. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/)
7. OpenAI acquired Odessia, a year-old AI travel agent founded by Sonder co-founder Francis Davidson, who is joining OpenAI to work on travel; terms were not disclosed, and the Odessia tool has already been shut down. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-acquires-odessia-travel-startup-created-by-sonder-founder/)
8. New York opened a registration portal for the RAISE Act, requiring large frontier AI developers such as OpenAI and Anthropic to register by January 1, 2027, publish safety frameworks, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours, and face penalties of up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for later ones. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/)
9. Google Cloud launched Gemini for healthcare in preview with partners including HCA Healthcare, Highmark Health, and Northwell Health, aiming to run multi-step clinical and administrative workflows such as medical-record synthesis and prior-authorization review with human oversight. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-for-healthcare/)
10. Some Independent Games Festival jurors published a statement saying they would not vote for games that use generative AI in any capacity, after which Untitled Goose Game studio House House said it used LLMs sparingly on Big Walk in the final stretch of development (estimating well under 0.1% of the code was involved) and apologized that this may not meet anti-AI standards. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1030994/big-walk-independent-games-festival-igf-ai)
