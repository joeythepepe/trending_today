## Top 10 AI News Today

1. Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by the new Gemini Decision 1 model, which can evaluate up to 1,000 yes/no, choice, and rubric-scoring questions in a single pass across text, images, audio, video, and PDFs, with 150-250 ms end-to-end latency at $0.10 per 1M input tokens and no output-token charges. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)
2. Google began rolling out the Gemini app on Apple CarPlay globally, letting iOS users have hands-free Gemini Live conversations from the car display, with chats that sync back to the iPhone app. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)
3. Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a next-generation DNA sequence model trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues that predicts how genetic variants affect specific human cell types; it identified nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as its predecessor, and is available for non-commercial research via the AlphaGenome API and in private preview for commercial research. [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/)
4. Google Labs launched Tabletop, a U.S. waitlist experiment (18+) that generates custom collaborative spaces such as trip itineraries, polls, expense splitters, and tournament brackets that groups can use together in real time. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/tabletop/)
5. United Airlines said it will not let unauthorized third-party AI agents book or complete transactions on its site or app, citing privacy, unintended purchases, and federal rules such as traveler attestations, though agents may still display United flights. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031623/united-unauthorized-ai-agents)
6. MiniMax released H3-Image as an open-weight image generation model that it says can produce a native 4K (4096 × 4096) image in under 10 seconds, with text-to-image, reference-based generation (up to 16 references), and image editing. [MiniMax](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3-image)
